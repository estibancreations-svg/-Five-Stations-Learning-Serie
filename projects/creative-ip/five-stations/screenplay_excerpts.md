# Five screenplay excerpts from the supplied package

These are **development excerpts**, one per series, adapted from the upload to correct lesson errors. They do not establish episode duration, shooting script completion, or a set of 30 finished samples. All exact teaching overlays require a literacy and science proofread before animatics.

## Momo S2E3 — The Cat That Couldn't Meow

```text
FADE IN:

EXT. MOONBEAN MEADOW - NIGHT

The flowers pulse softly. MOMO floats over a quiet CAT. The cat opens
its mouth. No sound.

MOMO
Where did your meow go?

TWINKLE
Maybe it's hiding in a cloud!

PHONO settles beside a wooden word board.

PHONO
Let's start with a word we can read. What do we call our friend?

On the board: C  A  T. The letters are a verified compositing overlay.

PHONO
Point to each sound. /k/ ... /ă/ ... /t/.

MOMO
/k/ ... /ă/ ... /t/. Cat!

The cat nudges the board and manages a tiny MEOW.

TWINKLE
It was listening for its name!

PHONO
We blended the word CAT. Your friend's meow is its own sound.

Momo reads CAT again as the cat curls beside the board.

FADE OUT.
```

## Rocket S3E2 — Rivet and the Lever Lift

```text
FADE IN:

EXT. TINY TOWN CONSTRUCTION SITE - DAY

A model BOULDER blocks the miniature playground road. ROCKET pushes.
His treads spin. RIVET stops him.

RIVET
Let's change how we push.

PROFESSOR PULLEY places a sturdy beam over a small support.

PROFESSOR PULLEY
The support is a fulcrum. Our rock is the load.

She sets the short end under the boulder. Rocket pushes down on the
long end. The boulder rises slightly.

ROCKET
My push traveled farther down. The rock moved a little way up.

PROFESSOR PULLEY
That's the trade. This arrangement lets you use less force over
a longer distance. Let's support the load before anyone goes near it.

Rivet wedges a safe support under the model rock. The road opens.

FADE OUT.
```

## Yum Yum Yetis S3E5 — Yum and the Cold Ice Cream

```text
FADE IN:

INT. SNACK MOUNTAIN KITCHEN - DAY

YUM carries a covered ice cream tub. NUM sets two empty bowls down.
MEASURE MOUSE watches the tub on a cool tray.

MEASURE MOUSE
Before we start, is this scoop holding its shape?

TINY
Yes. But the edge is getting soft.

Yum leaves one scoop in a sealed clear cup at room temperature while
an adult keeps a second sample in the freezer. A few minutes pass.

YUM
The first scoop is melting. It flows when I tip the cup.

MEASURE MOUSE
Heat moved into it from the warmer room. Some of the solid ice
crystals melted into liquid.

NUM
And the frozen sample still holds its shape. Let's compare them.

CHEF CRUMB
The sealed melted sample can go back in the freezer for our
next observation. Our snack comes from the safely stored tub.

They record two drawings: BEFORE and AFTER, added as checked overlays.

FADE OUT.
```

## Doodle S3E10 — Echo and the Force of Flight

```text
FADE IN:

INT. DREAMTOPIA SKETCHBOOK WORLD - DAY

DOODLE folds two paper planes. ECHO watches the first fall nose-first.

ECHO
They are both paper. Why did one glide?

DOODLE
Let's give them the same gentle launch and compare.

He throws the second. Its broad even wings carry it across the page.
COMPASS pins four arrows to the margin as an overlay.

COMPASS
The launch pushes it forward. Air resists its motion. Gravity
pulls it down. Air around its wings also helps support it.

MELODY
So the fold matters. Shall we change just one thing and test again?

Echo widens the wings of the first plane and marks a launch line.

FADE OUT.
```

## Power Pals S3E1 — Paige and the Geometry Gate

```text
FADE IN:

EXT. READER VALLEY - GEOMETRY GATE - DAY

A gate has four empty corners. A checked overlay presents the riddle:
FOUR EQUAL SIDES. FOUR RIGHT ANGLES.

PAIGE
Name a shape that fits both clues.

MAX
A square. A diamond-shaped rhombus could have four equal
sides but may not have right angles.

LINA
Let's measure. Equal bars first; ninety degrees at each corner.

KAI and ZOE position four glowing bars. Max checks each corner with
a square guide. The assembled square lights up; the gate opens.

ZOE
Both clues mattered. We used evidence to make the right shape.

PAIGE
Let's find another square on our map before we go.

FADE OUT.
```
